## The risk that they all lose together

- Same signals, same days: the three accounts' 6-month results are **84-91% correlated** (2026). The accounts differ only
  through their rules (daily loss, payout caps, floors).
- **Accounts blown together is rare:** every account loses a funded account in the same 6 months in 0.1% of 2026 paths
  (4.9% on 2021-25). That is because the funded sizing shrinks with the room and skips a trade that could reach the floor.
- **No money together is the real risk:** in 7.3% (H1) / 11.9% (H2) of paths, all three pay $0 in months 4-6, and 27-35% of
  paths have at least one account paying $0 then. The worst 10% of 6-month paths make $2,916 / $351 in total.
- **If 2027 looks like 2021-25** (LIT PF 1.01), all three lose fees together: −$92 a month on average, negative in 86% of
  6-month paths. A second firm protects against **firm** risk (a rule change, a payout denial, a firm closing), not against
  **strategy** risk.

## How it was measured

- **Trades:** the 120 live LIT trades of 2026 (run `lit_live`; 1m entries, stop 1 tick beyond the sweep extreme, VIX target
  banked at 8R), each with its stop and target distance, points, worst open loss and best open profit (for intraday
  floors), so each plan's size is applied path by path. $1.90 a micro round trip on every firm, 1 tick of slippage.
  Check: the MFFU Builder 50K funded mode gives $758 / $737 a month and 1.1 / 1.2% lost (`lit-funded-sizing.md`:
  $769 / $739, 1.0 / 1.5%, different paths).
- **Eval:** a fresh eval on every session of the half, played on that half's sessions only, no reset, up to 60 sessions
  (exact); and 2,000 Monte Carlo evals (5-session blocks). Rules per plan: target, EOD or intraday trailing floor with its
  lock, soft daily loss (flattened, day over), eval best-day rule (best day ≤ share × profit to pass), minimum days.
- **Funded:** 1,500 paths of 126 sessions (6 months) per half; each plan's payout rules (buffer, min / max per request by
  payout number, cycle profit, request spacing, winning days, best-day rule, split, payout count). A request takes
  everything above the buffer up to the cap.
- **6 months from the purchase ("life"):** one path: buy the eval; pass → funded; a failed eval or a lost / finished
  funded account → buy a new eval the next session. Net = payouts to you − every eval fee and activation.
- **Combos:** the plans on the same 1,500 paths (same trades), so the correlation is real, not assumed.
- **Picking:** eval sizing by H1 exact (eventual pass − eventual fail + ½ pass within 15 sessions); funded sizing by H1 MC
  ($ paid in 6 months − P(lost) × cost per funded account); the MFFU Builder keeps the live eval sizing and the documented
  funded mode. H2 is the check and is the number to quote.
- **Not modelled:** news rules except the Rapid worst case; payout processing days; a firm denying a payout; going live
  after 5 MFFU / Lucid payouts (the path just stops paying from that account and a new eval is bought); taxes.
- Code: `tools/lit_funded_firms.py` (`all`, then `save`). Numbers: `research/lit/funded_firms.json` (every plan's rules,
  sources, unconfirmed fields, the eval and funded grids, the picks, all combos).
