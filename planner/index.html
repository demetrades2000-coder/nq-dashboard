          How to use Planner

          Quick add

          Type in any task box. It understands dates, times, repeats and projects:

          • send invoice tomorrow #nq makes a task due tomorrow in NQ bot.
          • call Sam fri 3pm has a time, so it becomes a calendar event.
          • check bot logs daily repeats. Also weekdays and weekly.
          • Project tags: #kalshi #nq #jarvis #clip #port.

          Your day

          • Today is the home screen: schedule, news coming up, tasks, habits, health, trading and goals.
          • Plan day picks your tasks and puts them in free time. Plan week spreads them across Monday to Friday.
          • Drag an event to move it, drag its bottom edge to resize (press and hold on a phone). Swipe left or right to change day, week or month.
          • Tap the arrow on a task for steps, notes, a time estimate and Block time.

          Markets

          • Market layers on the calendar: high, medium and low USD releases, Fed speakers, mega-cap earnings and session shading. Turn them on or off in Market on the calendar.
          • News lists releases with forecast and previous, and headlines tagged risk-on or risk-off. Tap Refresh for the newest data your dashboard has published.
          • All market times are converted from New York time to your phone's time zone.
          • If a banner says the data is old, your bot PC has not published recently. Check the bots and ForexFactory directly.

          Discipline and money

          • Rules: your pre-trade checklist, run once per trading day. Trades: notes and tags on each live or paper trade, plus a payout planner.
          • Review: your week at a glance. Journal: note, mood and whether you followed your rules.
          • Projects holds Goals and the Money log. Health holds sleep, workouts and trends.
          • Week balance (card on Today): hours planned per event type against weekly targets you set, with free hours left after 8 h of sleep a day. Earnings (card on Today): estimated pay this week and month from shifts (hours x hourly rate), live and paper trading, and the money log, with a monthly income goal and what you need per week. Both are before tax.
          • Check my week (shield button, or the banner on Today) lists clashes for today and the next 7 days: events that overlap (red) and work shifts that run into a New York killzone or a high-impact news pause (amber). Tap one to open the event.
          • When can I trade today? on Today shows the free parts of each killzone after shifts, events and news pauses, plus the best free block of the week for deep work.
          • Bedtime (evening on Today, and a line in Plan day) takes tomorrow's first event, shift or marked trading session, subtracts your get-ready minutes and sleep hours, and says when to be asleep. Change the numbers with the Change button.
          • Bills (Today, or #bills): your recurring bills show on the calendar in red and feed a 30-day cash forecast that warns you before you run short. An estimate, before tax.
          • Tilt guard: after 2 losing trades in a row or your daily loss limit, a red Stop trading banner and a cooldown timer appear. Trade plan (before the NY open) turns into a debrief at 12 pm ET.
          • Close out the day (Today, from 5 pm): move open tasks to tomorrow, see your first commitment tomorrow, save one win.
          • Find (Ctrl/Cmd+K, or the Find button): searches everything and runs commands like new task call Sam fri, start clipper edit or next friday.
          • Time log: start a timer for what you are working on, or log time you forgot. Time audit compares what you planned with what you logged.
          • Deals: a board for leads, clients and sponsors with follow-up dates on the calendar, a Waiting on list, and a one-tap way to log the money from a won deal.
          • Edge finder (#edge): where you make and lose money by weekday, hour and tag, with plain findings. Small samples are greyed out. Daily score (#score): a 0-100 score from what you did, with streaks. Net worth (#worth): your accounts, savings goal and emergency fund, typed by hand.
          • Phone alerts (#alerts): makes a calendar file with reminders for shifts, events, bills and follow-ups. Open it on your iPhone and tap Add All, and Calendar will notify you. Miles and expenses (#miles): log trips and tools with a deduction estimate (your own numbers, not tax advice). Daily brief (#brief): a morning summary you can copy or share.
          • Payday (#payday): paydays on the calendar with what each should be, and a check that tells you if a paycheck looks short. Hours (#hours): weekly hours across jobs with overtime and rest warnings. Availability (#availability): copy a ready message for your manager and track days-off requests.
          • Prop firms (#prop): a ledger of every eval and funded account with fees, payouts, ROI and the payout still needed to break even. Business (#business): monthly profit and loss per project from your money log. Taxes (#taxes): a quarterly set-aside estimate with due dates on the calendar. Estimates only, not tax advice.
          • All tools (header button or #tools): every tool in one searchable list, with your recent and favorite tools first. Get set up on Today walks you through the settings that make the tools useful.

          Shortcuts and Back Tap

          In the iPhone Shortcuts app, add an Open URL action with this app's address plus one of these endings, then set Back Tap (Settings, Accessibility, Touch) to run it:

          • #today #plan #plan-week #new-event #new-task #shifts #week-balance #earnings
          • #log-workout #health #projects #week #agenda

          Your data

          • Everything is stored on this phone. Use Back up in Settings and save the file to iCloud Drive about once a week.
          • Calendar alerts exports a file you open in the iPhone Calendar app, which then sends alerts. It is a snapshot, so tap it again after big changes.
          • Reminders inside this app only appear while it is open. Apple does not let web apps send alerts on their own.
