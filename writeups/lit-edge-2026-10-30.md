## 4. What each idea showed, in plain words

- **Targets (ideas 1-3, 34 versions): the VIX band stays.** NQ's own daily range as the band gives the same kind of number,
  so most versions move money by a few hundred dollars a half. The one that helps Jul-Oct at the LIT size (5-day range x1.0:
  +$587) does it by moving 5 trades a half; on the eval it turns 2-week passes 52.2 -> 46.4% in Jul-Oct and 2-week fails 1.7
  -> 6.7% in Jan-Jun. Re-anchoring the band at the entry, the midnight open or the time left always costs Jan-Jun money
  ($0.5k-2.3k at the LIT size). The 2021-25 reference likes a wider reach (+$2-6k over five years) - the same lesson as the
  Asia-range targets of `lit-gap-audit.md`: helps the reference, not 2026.
- **Breakeven (ideas 4-5, 14 versions): 1R stays.** Waiting longer on tight stops (max(1R, x)) kills Jan-Jun winners
  (-$0.9k to -$8.3k); breaking even sooner on wide stops (min(1R, x)) kills Jul-Oct winners (-$0.4k to -$7.3k; the 4-ATR
  version changes nothing). LIT winners dip before they run.
- **Dynamic targets (ideas 6-8, 47 versions).** Lifting the bank needs the one +$6,176 trend day of 2026-03-18 (8R -> 18.6R)
  and gives back 2026-01-22 (6.5R -> breakeven); at 10 minutes nothing changes in Jul-Oct, at 30 minutes the Jan-Jun eval
  loses $8.1k, and the funded account loses $143 / $52 a month (bigger days vs the 50% best-day rule). Slow-trade targets help
  2021-25 (up to +$12k) and lose or do nothing in Jul-Oct. The afternoon cap is the most coherent family: **10 of 12
  versions gain in Jul-Oct** (+$311 to +$473) and **9 of 12 in 2021-25**, but Jan-Jun is split (8 of 12 lose on the eval
  path) and Jul-Oct rests on one trade (2026-09-18, breakeven -> +3R). The 14:00 / 3R version: LIT +$460 / +$311 / +$1,483,
  eval +$606 / +$3,913, funded +$15 / +$58 a month, 2-week pass 59.7 / 53.6% with fails unchanged.
- **15-minute check in ATR (idea 9, 15 versions): 0.3R stays.** The best Jan-Jun version (0.5 ATR) changes 3 Jul-Oct trades
  for the worse (-$1,302, funded -$168 a month); the safe one (the smaller of 0.3R and 0.75 ATR) changes a single trade
  (2026-05-05, +$192).
- **Size tilts (ideas 10-12, 16 versions + 200 placebo draws each): none beats chance.** The pre-registered A+ score is
  **backwards in Jan-Jun** (A x1.5 + C x0.5: -$5,019) and in 2021-25 (-$5,300); only "A x1.5" makes money, and random
  trades sized up as often make more 93% of the time in Jan-Jun. On the eval path every tilt that cuts size loses passes
  (two-week sizing needs the full size to pass with one winner: e.g. 2+ inducements x1.5 / else x0.75 drops 2-week passes
  57.1 -> 47.1% and 52.2 -> 31.9%). On the **funded account the size-ups move only -$16 to +$16 a month**, because the
  $1,500 risk cap and the $2,000 winner cap absorb them, while the cuts cost real money (A+ x1.5 / C x0.5: -$94 / -$109 a
  month). One curiosity: "busy tape" (5m ATR vs the VIX move in the top third, x1.25) beats 99.5% of placebos in Jul-Oct and
  makes +$1,707 / +$1,333 / +$578, but in Jan-Jun it is no better than random sizing (65%) and it nudges Jan-Jun 2-week fails
  1.7 -> 2.5%: not proven. The mined weekday (Fri x1.5 / Tue x0.5) loses -$2,609 in Jul-Oct, as curve fits do.
- **Frequency (ideas 13-15).** The weekly catch-up barely fires: in 2026 only 3 Thu/Fri days lacked a trade and had a looser
  setup, so trades a week in Jul-Oct go 2.56 -> 2.69 for -$122. Re-entry after the cut or a breakeven adds 30 trades in 2026
  that lose 3.35R together (15 stop-outs). A 2nd trade a day loses Jan-Jun money in every version (-$1.0k to -$5.0k at the
