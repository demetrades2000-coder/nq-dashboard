## How it is kept

- Data: `research/props/firms.json` (edit it when a rule changes; keep `st` and `src`), `research/props/business.json`
  (your fees and payouts, goal, tax %), `research/props/earnings.json` (mega-cap dates). An Excel file
  `research/business/prop_business.xlsx` (sheets expenses / payouts) overrides the business JSON when present.
- Code: `tools/hub_data.py` (all numbers, read-only), `engine/hub_api.py` (GET `/api/next/hub/*`), page
  `web_next/js/next/hubpages.js`. `python tools/hub_data.py` writes `research/props/hub.json`, which the cloud dashboard
  build passes through as `data.json` "hub" for the phone.
- LIT numbers: `research/lit/funded_firms.json` (tools/lit_funded_firms.py). Eval odds on the Business page: the stored
  Monte Carlo paths (`research/lab/montecarlo.json`) conditioned on today's profit and room.
- Not modelled: payout processing days, a firm refusing a payout, taxes beyond a flat set-aside (not tax advice).
