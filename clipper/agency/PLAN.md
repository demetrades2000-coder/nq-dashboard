# Clip agency plan (v1, Oct 27 2026)

Decisions: sell clipping to trading / prop firms, solo with automation, current bot stays on but not posting.
All numbers below are my assumptions, not measured. Test them with the first 3 clients.

## 1. The offer (one product, three tiers)
"We turn your long videos into 60+ short clips a month, posted on dedicated pages, with a weekly report."
| Tier | Price / month | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $750 | 1 TikTok page, ~30 clips + slideshows, weekly report |
| Growth | $1,500 | 3 pages (TikTok + Shorts + Reels), ~90 posts, weekly report, hooks tuned to their brand |
| Scale | $3,000 | 6+ pages, daily posting, monthly strategy call |
Pilot: 14 days at $250, credited to month 1 if they continue. Cancel any time.
Why this works for prop firms: they already pay creators per 1,000 views on Content Rewards (Tradever pays $5 CPM). A flat fee that is cheaper than the views they would buy is an easy yes.

## 2. Cost to serve (per client)
Cloud runs are free. Costs: Zernio about $6 per page past the first 2, TikTok accounts free, your time. Target margin 85%+.
At 5 Growth clients: $7,500/mo revenue, roughly $100 to $200 cost.

## 3. Getting clients (the part that decides everything)
1. Build a list of 50 prop firms and trading educators already running clipping campaigns (the bot's LEADS scan has 408 campaigns; filter finance).
2. For each of the top 20, make a free sample: 5 clips from their public video, posted nowhere, sent as a Drive link.
3. DM / email the sample to the marketing person: "5 clips from your last video, free. Want 60 a month for $750?"
4. Goal: 20 samples sent, 5 calls, 2 pilots in the first 2 weeks.
Needed: a one-page site (use the dashboard's public page style), 3 sample reels, a short contract.

## 4. What I build next (in order)
1. Multi-client mode: one workspace per client, own brand logo / spelling / banned phrases, own pages, one combined dashboard (the lifecycle code already supports many campaigns; clients become "private campaigns" with a flat fee instead of CPM).
2. Client report: auto weekly PDF/page (posts, views, best clip, next week plan) from the existing insights.
3. Client portal link: read-only dashboard per client (hides other clients, hides your costs).
4. Sample maker: paste a YouTube link, get 5 branded clips in a folder (for outreach).
5. Billing tracker: who is paying, renewals, churn alerts to your phone.
6. Pitch assets: site, deck, contract, onboarding form.

## 5. Risks (honest)
- No client has said yes yet; sales is the real work, not the software.
- Clients' rules / platform policies on automated posting vary; get written permission per client (the bot's permission gate already enforces this).
- TikTok may limit new pages posted by API; warm-up mode exists but is untested.
- Quality: clips still need a human spot check for new clients in the first week.

## 6. 30-day timeline
Week 1: multi-client mode + sample maker + list of 50 leads. Week 2: outreach, 20 samples. Week 3: calls, 2 pilots start. Week 4: reports + portal, convert pilots, aim for $1.5k to $3k MRR.
