# NARRATIVE RESULTS: the trader brain (engine/narrative.py), 16 Oct 2026

All numbers come from real CME NQ1!/ES1! 5m bars. Every table, with confidence intervals, is in `NARRATIVE_RESULTS_tables.md`, which is regenerated by `python -X utf8 tools/lab_narrative_run.py report`. The rules were pre-registered in `NARRATIVE_RULES.md`: v1 before any outcome was seen, v2 in section 7. The thought logs are in `NARRATIVE_EXAMPLES.md` and the monthly learning log is in `NARRATIVE_EXPERIENCE_LOG.md`.

## Bottom line

1. **The brain works and explains itself. Its grades still do not rank trades.**
   - It builds a pre-market story, judges each setup with reasons, and manages the trade bar by bar with a reason at every step.
   - On the engine's 2026 picks, the v1 grades run backwards in both halves:
     - dev: A -0.44R (n=10), B +0.07, C +0.17; Spearman -0.12
     - confirm: A +0.48 (n=5), B +1.12, C +0.82; Spearman -0.20
   - On the 2025 check, A is the best grade (A +0.24, B -0.24, C -0.10) but the order is not monotone (Spearman +0.08).
   - On the engine's 987 trades from 2021-2026, Spearman is -0.04.
   - v2 is no better: Spearman -0.36 on dev, -0.01 on confirm, +0.06 on 2025.
   - This repeats the earlier A..D finding: on this data, setup grades do not rank trades.
2. **Learning from experience did not fix the ranking.** Each element's trust was re-weighted monthly from past trades (walk-forward), and the grades still did not rank out of sample:
   - Spearman -0.11 on dev, -0.14 on confirm, +0.07 on 2025.
   - What it learned mostly says the expected effects are absent. For example, by Feb 2026 "with the bias" trades had averaged -0.12R against +0.10R for counter-bias trades, so it cut the bias weight to x0.47.
3. **Sizing by grade costs money and trades.** On the 2026 engine trades:

   | readout | net | PF | max DD | trades/wk | 50K pass (zero-edge) |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
   | engine as is | +$11,630 | 2.41 | -$3,147 | 2.26 | 48.1% (27.0%) |
   | v1, drop C/SKIP, A $700 / B $350 | +$2,707 | 1.33 | -$3,785 | 1.0 | 44.9% (34.8%) |
   | v1, keep C at $175 | +$5,067 | 1.53 | -$4,254 | 1.94 | 45.5% (36.0%) |
   | v2, keep C at $175 | +$5,159 | 1.55 | -$5,762 | 1.5 | 32.4% (22.7%) |

   - Losing months go from 5 to 6.
   - On 2025, "drop" is +$1,431 against the base +$1,361, at 1.69/wk.
   - The A-grade trades were the worst group in 2026, so tiering puts the most money on them.
4. **The brain's own trade management (Alt-3) is worse overall.**
   - 2026: +$5,430 against the engine's own exits at +$11,630.
   - dev: +$1,459 against +$592 (better).
   - confirm: +$3,971 against +$11,038.
   - 2025: -$182 against +$1,361.
   - The 60-minute "no progress" cut and the first-draw exits cut the Aug-Oct trend winners short.
5. **Daily bias (owner request): with-bias trades are not better on the engine.** I tested 6 bias definitions.
   - Engine 2025-26 (211 trades):
     - brain bias: aligned -0.10R (n=43), counter +0.16R (n=55)
     - TTrades closure: aligned -0.05R (91), counter +0.24R (94)
   - Engine 2021-24 (711 trades): every definition lands within +/-0.07R of zero.
   - On the three ICT lab models, with-bias trades did better. The best-sampled definition is **the 1H BOS (with the 1H order flow)**, with about 40-115 trades per side:
     - Silver Bullet: +0.25R
     - Mech/IFVG: +0.45R
     - ICT2022: +0.35R
   - The brain's composite bias points the same way (+0.34R on Silver Bullet, +0.31R on Mech/IFVG), but on thin groups: Mech/IFVG has only 9 aligned trades and ICT2022 only 2 counter-bias trades.
   - Two cautions on that result:
     - It re-measures the same model beds where CONFLUENCE_RESULTS found `trend_1h_with` (+0.30R pooled), so it is not an independent replication.
     - It does not survive the multiple-testing line.
   - `require_bias_alignment=True` with the brain's bias, on the engine:
     - 2026: +$9,333 at 1.58/wk, against +$11,630 at 2.26/wk.
     - 2025: -$2,192, against +$1,361.
   - It is **off by default**.
6. **The narrative does not forecast the day.** Over all 2022-2026 sessions:
   - The pre-market bias matched the RTH direction 48-52.5% of the time. High conviction did not help (46-56%).
   - The primary draw was hit 50-55% of the time, about the same as the nearest pool on the opposite side (45-58%).
   - "Expansion" days had *smaller* RTH ranges (0.69-0.84 ADR) than "consolidation" days (0.80-1.19).
   - This matches TRADER_HEURISTICS' own NQ checks: bias says which level is drawn next, not how the day closes, and compression-to-expansion fails on NQ.
7. **Other models:**
   - Silver Bullet, Mech/IFVG and ICT2022: no grade ranks in both halves.
     - Silver Bullet v1: Spearman +0.24 / +0.11 / +0.03, but its A grade is 3 trades.
     - ICT2022 v1 orders A > B > C on dev, but not on confirm or 2025.
   - The ATM model, on the ATM agent's saved `run()` output for its 1m ladder (57 trades, 2026 only):
     - v1's A grade is the best group in both halves: dev A +0.55R (n=10), confirm A +0.32R (n=3).
     - B < C in both halves, so the order is not monotone.
     - Spearman is +0.02 on dev and +0.34 on confirm.
     - The samples are far too thin to act on.
   - The owner's own ATM tiers on the same trades:
     - dev: A +0.28, B -0.19, C +1.08
     - confirm: A +0.03, B -0.37, C -1.02
   - The ATM 5m `run()` defaults produced only 11 trades in 18 months, too few to grade.
8. **Recommendation:**
   - Use the brain to **explain, journal and plan**: the pre-market story, the reasons at entry and the reasons during the trade.
   - Use its hard vetoes as **risk rules** only. News windows, lunch and the first 15 minutes are the owner's call, and each costs trades.
   - Do **not** let the brain size or skip trades by grade. Keep flat $350.
   - Keep `require_bias_alignment` off. If the owner wants one alignment rule for the ICT-style models (SB/MECH/ICT2022), the 1H BOS is the only candidate the data lean toward. Paper-trade it before going live.

## Multiple testing (be skeptical of any single cell)

- **Scored readouts.** The primary is v1, plus the 3 pre-registered alternatives: v2, experience, and brain management. All are reported above, with no picking.
- **Descriptive extras.** These were run on request and nothing was selected from them:
  - score-only grades
  - other models
  - 6 bias definitions x 6 beds
  - a 26-element x 6-bed map
  - Among roughly 156 element-by-bed cells, several |diffs| of 0.3R or more appear by luck.
- **Element map (tables section 8).**
  - No element has the same sign on all 6 beds.
  - The most consistent are "with the 1H order flow" (5 of 6 positive, mostly small) and "with the IB side after 10:30" (5 of 5 non-thin beds positive).
  - Strong displacement into the entry is *negative* on most beds. This echoes CONFLUENCE `liq_void_draw` and `atr_expanding`.
  - Read the map as hypotheses for paper trading, not as rules.
- **Priors from overlapping data.** v1's weights used priors from CONFLUENCE_RESULTS and DRAW_LEARNER, which were measured on overlapping 2025-26 data. So the v1 test is not perfectly blind, and it still failed.
- **v2 was written after v1's result was known.** It was still written before any v2 run. Its first-15-minute veto alone removes 25 of 93 engine trades in 2026, which takes v2 below 2 trades a week on that bed.

## API for the trader layer (all models)

Interfaces are documented at the top of `engine/narrative.py`. All inputs are plain dicts and DataFrames.

- `narrative(bars5, t, es5=None, bias_source=None)`
- `assess(setup, nar=None, bars5=..., es5=None, version="v1", trust=None, require_bias_alignment=False)`
  - Required setup keys: `entry_t`, `dir`, `entry`, `stop`.
  - Optional setup keys: `sweep_level`, `sweep_label` (or `label`), `sweep_t`, `trigger_tf`, `targets`, `model`.
  - Without targets, it uses the nearest untaken Tier-A pool at least 1R away, else 2R.
- `manage(position, bars_since_entry, nar=None)`
- `simulate(...)`
- `infer_sweep(...)` (sweep detection for any model)
- `enable_v2()`
- `register(...)` (plug in new heuristics)
- `use_daily_bias(method)` returns a `bias_source` backed by `engine/daily_bias.py` once that module exists. It is due about 22:00, so it is not tested here. Switch to it only if its BIAS_REPORT ranking beats the brain's bias.
