## What I would join first and why

1. **TJR ($23.1k weekly)**: the only open listing that is large, uncrowded per dollar, long-form-fed and in our niche. Post 1 test clip to see if approvals come inside 24 h and whether a fresh account reaches 5,000 views.
2. **Clavicular (Clip Farm)** and **FOLD** in parallel: both have a 1,000-view minimum, real proof of payouts ($42 and $22 per approved clip) and months of runway. These are the likeliest to actually pay a new clipper something in week 1.
3. **BILF Podcast** only for the first 10 days (it will likely drain), and **Dovy fx** for trading long-form (read its 9 requirements first).
4. **Vest Markets** and **FOMO TOKEN2049** are cheap lottery tickets: great rate and budget but zero proof; test one clip each, expand only if approvals land.

Realistic expectation: a new TikTok account probably nets $0 to $300 per campaign in the first 2 to 4 weeks. Money comes from (a) the minimum-payout hurdle being low (1,000 to 2,000 views), (b) volume across 3 to 4 campaigns that accept the same clip, and (c) the account gaining reach. The CPM matters less than the minimum.
