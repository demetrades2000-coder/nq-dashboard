# Owner's LIT notes (screenshots from the owner's LIT study group, 2026-10-18)

Paraphrased rules to test. LIT only; judged on 2026 (pick Jan-Jun, check Jul-Oct).

## Mitigation (order blocks / POIs)
- Mitigation = price filling inefficiencies and delivering evenly.
- The strongest area of interest is the mitigation (OB) left by the **initial BoS from a peak** (the first break of
  structure after the swing high/low that started the move).
- If liquidity sits nearby, the market **will induce first, then mitigate**: only zones whose nearby liquidity has
  been taken are selective, valid mitigations.
- A valid mitigation **must have an inefficiency (FVG) above or below it**.
- Two boxes on the charts: "Mitigation" (the full origin range) and "Refined" (a smaller box inside it, near the
  last opposing candle / the FVG edge) used for the entry.
- **Orderflow mitigation:** after a peak, each lower high (or higher low) leaves a new supply (demand) block; price
  steps down mitigating them one after the other, then delivers.

## SMT = Smart Money Trap (not NQ/ES divergence)
- A false reaction off a supply/demand zone that then fails.
- If there is liquidity right below a demand mitigation (or right above a supply one), the zone is most likely a trap.
- Traps mark later inflection points and show where liquidity is.
- Trade a trap only with strong confluence, mainly HTF, with LTF entries.

## F-BoS (false break of structure) and the build-up
- **16:30-17:00 (ET, before the daily halt) is the build-up window:** look for one side to be F-BoS'd right away; it
  sometimes starts the whole Asia run.
- No build-up: expect a continuation of the LIT cycle that was running before the Asia open.
- HTF build-up before Asia = HTF IDM, HTF mitigation, HTF F-BoS.
- Examples are shown at the **9:30** open and the **18:00** open: a build-up, a false break of one side, then the run.

## Sessions
- Asia IDM -> London and pre-NY deliver continuation.
- London or Frankfurt IDM -> NY continues London's direction.
- No IDM before NY -> expect a sharp IDM move in NY, then decisive delivery.

## R-BoS (real break of structure)
- Don't rely on an R-BoS to enter: NQ often runs without giving an entry. (Matches the entry study: waiting for a 1m
  BOS was worse than the 1m CISD in both halves, `lit-entry-precision.md`.)

## Tips (second batch of screenshots)
- Someone is always being induced, in every direction: find where, and whose stops.
- Make sure the "inducement" isn't a build-up of equal highs/lows: real inducement is taken **fast**.
- Watch the Asia high/low, PDH/PDL, HTF build-ups and the time.
- Always trade **away from** the inducement. Inducements rank minor -> medium -> major: prioritise the higher one.
- Session opens often give the **final** inducement.
- "Always ask who is profiting right now and where their stops are."

## LIT visuals
- **Trendline / channel liquidity (TS):** a falling (rising) channel of lower highs and lows; the channel's last low
  is swept later ("TS" line), then the run the other way. A wedge of lower highs breaks, the last high leaves a supply
  block, price retraces into it and drops.
- **Demand to supply (flip):** a demand zone that fails becomes supply; the retest from below is the short entry
  (and the mirror for supply to demand).
- **Continuation inducement:** in a trend, an internal low (high) is swept against the trend, then the trend continues.
- **Reversal inducement:** a channel of rising lows under equal highs; the highs are swept, then the reversal.
- **SMT picture:** a rising channel with a supply zone above. Price wicks into the supply and fails (false reaction);
  the channel low (F-BoS line) is swept; then the strong rally.
- **FVG + compression:** after a break to a peak, price pulls back in a curved, compressing channel and returns into
  the FVG / supply at the peak, then drops.
